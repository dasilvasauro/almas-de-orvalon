# Souls of Orvalon — Rulebook

> Orvalon was one kingdom while Aldren, the Last King, lived. On the night he died without heirs, the Iron Crown shattered, and every shard bound itself to the soul of a hero or monster from one of the kingdom’s lands. The Five Gods awoke and made a decree: whoever gathers the Souls in the Coliseum of Valcrown shall rule Orvalon. Since then, Summoners have bound those souls into Seals — the cards — and duel for them.

## 1. Welcome, Summoner

Orvalon was one kingdom until the death of Aldren, the Last King. The Iron Crown shattered, and each shard bound itself to the soul of a hero or monster. The Five Gods decreed: whoever gathers the Souls in the Coliseum shall rule.

Each match is a duel between two **Summoners** with **40-card** decks. Each starts with **4 Souls** face down. Every time you defeat an opponent’s creature, you take 1 Soul into your hand — or **2** if the creature is a **Legend**.

You win in two ways: **claiming all your Souls** or leaving the opponent with **no creatures in play**. If someone’s deck runs out, every turn they can’t draw, the opponent claims 1 Soul (**Exhaustion**).

_Example cards: Sir Aldric Varenne, Vorgul_

## 2. Card types

Each type has its own frame color. **Creatures** (bronze) are Heroes and Monsters that fight. **Charges** (obsidian) pay for attacks. **Effects** (green) are spells: Instant ones resolve at once; Lasting ones stay active until the end of your next turn.

**Relics** (wine) attach to a creature and grant powers. **Actions** (blue) are strong plays — only 1 per turn. **Gods** (gold) reign in the Dominion and empower an affinity for both sides. **Markers** (silver) activate on their own when they enter your hand.

_Example cards: Sister Selene, Ember Charge, Priest’s Blessing, Crimson River, Runic Saw, Pact of Spirits, Kragmor, Heart of Thorns_

## 3. Anatomy of a creature

- **HP**: how much damage it can take. When damage reaches its HP, it’s defeated.
- **Affinity**: Ember, Sacred, Occult, Storm or Primal (the orb in the corner).
- **Awakened** or **Ascended**: Awakened ones enter play directly; Ascended ones evolve an Awakened.
- **Gift**: an ability. Some are passive; others you activate once per turn.
- **Attacks**: the orbs on the left are the Charge cost; the number on the right is the damage.
- **Weakness** (+30 damage), **Resistance** (−20) and **Retreat** (Charges to swap places).

_Example cards: Mother Selene, Pyrrhax_

## 4. Setting up

**Step by step**

1. Both players shuffle. A coin decides who goes first (Sun or Moon).
2. Each draws 6 cards. Markers in the opening hand go back to the deck and are replaced.
3. No Awakened creature in hand? **Mulligan**: reveal it, shuffle everything back and draw 6 again. The opponent draws 1 extra card per mulligan of yours (max 3).
4. Choose 1 Awakened creature as your **Active** and up to 4 for your **Reserve**.
5. Set aside the top 4 cards of your deck, face down: they are your **Souls**.

## 5. Your turn

**Turn order**

1. **Draw 1 card.** If your deck is empty, instead of drawing, the opponent claims 1 Soul (Exhaustion).
2. **Main phase**, in any order and as often as you like within the limits: put Awakened creatures in the Reserve (max 4); Ascend; attach **1 Charge** from hand; play Effects; play **1 Action**; attach Relics (1 per creature); play **1 God**; use Gifts; **Retreat once**.
3. **Attack** with your Active. Attacking ends your turn. You may also end without attacking.
4. **Between Turns**: Conditions and end-of-turn effects resolve.

First-turn rule: the starting player **can’t attack or play an Action** on turn 1. No one Ascends on their own first turn.

## 6. Charges and costs

Colored orbs in a cost require a Charge of that affinity. The gray (**Neutral**) orb accepts any Charge. Primal creatures only have Neutral costs: they accept any Charge but hit a little softer. A Neutral Charge also heals 10 when attached to a Primal.

**Example**

1. Vorgul has Ember + Ember + Neutral attached.
2. Eruption costs Ember, Ember and Neutral: it can be used.
3. If he had Ember + Sacred + Sacred, he **couldn’t**: the second Ember is missing (Sacred only pays the Neutral cost).

_Example cards: Vorgul, Ember Charge, Neutral Charge_

## 7. Calculating damage

- 1. Printed damage + the attack’s own effects (coins, bonuses).
- 2. Bonuses from Relics, Lasting Effects, Gifts, God and Markers — only if step 1 damage is above 0.
- 3. **Weakness**: +30 damage.
- 4. **Resistance**: −20.
- 5. Defender reductions (Gifts, Relics, Effects). Damage never goes below 0.

Weakness cycle: **Sacred** beats Occult → **Occult** beats Primal → **Primal** beats Storm → **Storm** beats Ember → **Ember** beats Sacred.

**Calculation example**

1. Kael (Ember) uses Blazing Dive: 100.
2. He holds a Runic Saw (+20) and Kragmor reigns in the Dominion (+20 for Ember): 140.
3. The target, Sir Aldric (Sacred), is weak to Ember: +30 = 170.
4. Aldric has 110 HP and no reductions: he takes 170 and is defeated. Kael claims 1 Soul.

Damage to **Reserve** creatures has no Weakness, Resistance, bonuses or reductions — only a Reserve protection prevents it. “Putting damage” (from Effects or Gifts) isn’t modified either.

## 8. Retreating and switching

To **Retreat**, discard Charges from your Active equal to its Retreat cost and swap it with a Reserve creature. Only once per turn. The game discards Neutral Charges first, then those of another affinity.

You can’t retreat while Stunned, Entranced or Sealed. Cards that **switch** (like Broken Wings) ignore those locks. When it goes to the Reserve, the creature loses Conditions and temporary effects, but keeps its damage.

## 9. Conditions

- **Poison**: 20 damage Between Turns.
- **Burn**: 20 damage Between Turns; then flip a coin — Sun heals it.
- **Trance**: can’t attack or retreat. Between Turns, flip a coin — Sun wakes it.
- **Stun**: can’t attack or retreat during its owner’s next turn; then it ends.
- **Madness**: when attacking, flip a coin — on Moon the attack fails and the creature takes 30.

Some attacks **Weaken** (the opponent’s Active does less damage during their next turn) or **Expose** (the opponent’s Active takes more damage until the end of your next turn). They don’t stack: the highest applies. They end if the creature goes to the Reserve.

Trance, Stun and Madness replace each other; Poison and Burn stack with them. Only the Active can have Conditions.

## 10. Ascension

Play an Ascended creature on top of the Awakened form named on it. The creature keeps damage, Charges and Relic, loses Conditions and gains the new HP, Gift and attacks. The Awakened one must have been in play since before this turn, and each creature ascends only once per turn.

**Example**

1. Turn 3: Sister Selene is in the Reserve with 30 damage.
2. You play Mother Selene on top of her.
3. Mother Selene has 120 HP and keeps the 30 damage: 90 left.

_Example cards: Sister Selene, Mother Selene_

## 11. Effects, Relics, Actions, Gods and Markers

- A card that would have no effect at all can’t be played (e.g. healing with no one damaged).
- **Lasting Effects**: up to 2 active at once, never two with the same name. They last until the end of your next turn.
- **Relics**: 1 per creature. They go to the discard pile with it.
- **Actions**: only 1 per turn.
- **Gods**: only 1 reigns in the Dominion. A new God sends the previous one to its owner’s discard. It affects both players.
- **Markers**: when they enter your hand (draw, search or Soul), they activate, go to the discard pile and you draw 1 card.
- Gifts with the same name don’t stack.

_Example cards: Broken Wings, Bone Reliquary, Runic Sealing, Aurelia, Constellation Die_

## 12. Legends, Souls and victory

**Legends** are powerful, but give up **2 Souls** when defeated. Use them carefully.

- Souls victory: claim your last Soul.
- Scorched-earth victory: the opponent has no creature to promote to Active.
- Note: gathering the last Soul ends the match **even if the loser still has creatures in play**.
- **Exhaustion**: whoever can’t draw at the start of their turn gives the opponent 1 Soul.
- **Sudden Death**: if both meet a condition at the same time, whoever gathered more Souls wins; if tied, it’s a draw.
- **Limit**: after 60 total turns, whoever has fewer Souls left wins.

_Example cards: Auryel, Azhur_

## 13. Building a deck

- Exactly 40 cards, with at least 1 Awakened creature.
- Up to 3 copies of each card (**variants** count together with the original); Legends and Gods, up to 2. Basic Charges are unlimited. Maximum of 4 Markers.
- Suggested ratio: 13–16 creatures, 12–15 Charges and 10–13 support cards.
- Stick to 1 or 2 affinities. Primal fits with any of them.
- Include draw and switch cards. The builder’s analysis tells you what’s missing.

## 14. A full example turn

**Your turn 4**

1. You draw: it’s the **Astral Drop** (Marker). It activates: attach a Charge from your discard to your Active, Cinzal. You draw 1 card to replace it.
2. You put **Isolde** in the Reserve. Her Gift searches your deck for an Awakened creature.
3. You play **Pact of Spirits** (your Action for the turn) and draw 3.
4. You attach 1 **Ember Charge** to Cinzal: now he can use Pack Rush.
5. Attack: 30 + 20 (another Pack creature is in play) = 50. Is the opponent’s Active Storm? No Weakness applies. It had 40 HP left: **defeated**.
6. You claim 1 Soul. The opponent chooses a new Active from their Reserve.
7. Between Turns: the opponent’s new Active has no Conditions; nothing happens. End of turn.

_Example cards: Astral Drop, Isolde, Ignarr, Pact of Spirits_

## Quick rules

- Goal: claim your 4 Souls by defeating creatures (Legends are worth 2) or leave the opponent with no creatures. Empty deck: every turn without a draw, the opponent claims 1 Soul.
- Turn: draw 1 → play cards → attack (ends the turn).
- Per-turn limits: 1 Charge, 1 Action, 1 God, 1 Retreat. Effects and creatures: as many as you like.
- The starting player can’t attack or play an Action on turn 1. No one Ascends on their own first turn.
- Damage: base → bonuses → Weakness +30 → Resistance −20 → reductions.
- Weaknesses: Sacred > Occult > Primal > Storm > Ember > Sacred.
- Conditions: Poison 20 · Burn 20 (Sun heals) · Trance (Sun wakes) · Stunned (1 turn) · Madness (Moon fails and takes 30).
- Reserve: up to 4 creatures. Tap a card for details; drag from your hand to play.
